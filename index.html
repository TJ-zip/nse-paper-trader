How it works

1. Virtual capital

You start with ₹10,00,000 of virtual cash. Nothing is real, no signup, no money at risk.

2. Live prices

Quotes for NSE stocks and indices are pulled from a public market data feed and refresh every few seconds during market hours (9:15 AM – 3:30 PM IST, Mon–Fri).

3. Realistic fills

Market orders fill at the last traded price. Limit orders wait in the book. Brokerage of ₹20 per executed order is deducted, like a discount broker.

4. Track performance

Positions, average price, unrealised and realised P&L are computed live and stored in your browser.

Disclaimer: This is an educational simulator. Prices are sourced from free public endpoints, may be delayed or approximate, and must not be used for real investment decisions. If the live feed is unreachable, the app switches to a clearly-labelled simulated feed so you can keep practising.
